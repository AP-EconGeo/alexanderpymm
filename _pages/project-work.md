Alongside my research, I also work on the operational side of funded project delivery, spanning project management and administration across the grant lifecycle. I'm particularly interested in finding more efficient and effective ways of delivering this work with the use of modern technologies and digital workflows.
This page is a collection of recent projects I've worked on to accomplish that.

## Programme budget and funding pipeline

*A worked demonstration, August 2026*

Three files that together show the infrastructure sitting underneath a grant-funded programme: a costed budget with compliance tests and a variance-flagged burn tracker, a risk register, and a screening register that turns public funder pages into a dated, sourced list of next actions.

- **[Cover note (PDF, 2pp)]({{ site.baseurl }}/files/Cover_Note_Programme_Budget_and_Funding_Pipeline.pdf)** — what the pack is, how the programme's scale was derived from published award precedents, what is real and what is invented, and what I would do differently with access to internal cost data. Read this first.
- **[Programme budget model (Excel, 5 tabs)]({{ site.baseurl }}/files/SHOC2_Illustrative_Programme_Budget_Model_v1.xlsx)** — assumptions, budget by workstream and financial year, donor-format cost summary, forecast-versus-actual burn tracker with variance flags, and a risk register. Built to a real funder's published cost rules: seven budget lines, a 20% overhead cap, a 10% capital cap, monitoring and evaluation at 5 to 10% of total, a ±10% budget-line variance threshold, and no contingency line, which that funder does not permit. Every compliance test recalculates from the inputs.
